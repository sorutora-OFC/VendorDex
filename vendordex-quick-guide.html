VendorDEX

TCG Inventory · Sales · Shows

The binder that keeps up with your business — every card, every sale, every show, tracked in one place.

Built for TCG collectors and vendors who are done guessing what's in the box, what it's worth, or what to bring to the next show. Here's what VendorDEX does for you.

01 / 06

Dashboard — your business at a glance

Log in and see exactly where you stand: total market value of your inventory, what's in stock, and profit for the month — updated live. A performance chart tracks your profit from a single day out to a full year, and an "Urgent — Sell" alert quietly flags cards that have been sitting too long, before they become dead stock.

  • Gross value, stock count & monthly profit
  • Interactive profit chart, 1D to 1Y
  • Automatic aging alerts
  • Your top 3 recent sales
02 / 06

Add cards your way

Getting a card into your binder never means fighting a spreadsheet. Point your camera at it and let VendorDEX identify it and pull pricing automatically. Search the database by name. Or type in every detail yourself — condition, grading, cost, all of it — when you want full control.

  • Scan Card — camera + auto lookup
  • Search Cards — live database search
  • Add Manually — every field, your call
  • Look Up Price — check value on demand
03 / 06

The Store — buy, sell, track profit

This is where inventory turns into revenue. Browse Active cards in a clean grid or list, sort by price or set, and mark anything SOLD in one tap — VendorDEX logs the sale price and works out your profit for you. Every sale and removal lands in Inactive with full history, and Total Sales keeps a running ledger of everything that's come in.

  • Sortable grid & list views
  • One-tap SOLD, profit calculated instantly
  • Full sales history with date filtering
  • Export any view to CSV or PDF
04 / 06

All Inventory — the whole collection, at a glance

Zoom out and see active and sold cards side by side, automatically grouped and counted. A running value chart shows how your collection has grown over time, and Collection Allocation breaks everything into Graded, Raw, and Sealed so you know exactly what your portfolio is made of. Built-in search finds any card in seconds — tap a result to open its full details.

  • Combined active + sold view with quantities
  • Historical inventory value chart
  • Graded / Raw / Sealed allocation
  • Instant search & 61+ day aging flags
05 / 06

Schedule — never show up unprepared

Plan every show on a full calendar, then let "What Should I Bring?" do the thinking — it compares your sales history against your current stock and tells you exactly what, and how much value, to pack. PSA/TAG submissions and giveaways live right alongside your shows, so nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Full show calendar
  • Smart "What Should I Bring?" packing lists
  • PSA/TAG submission tracker
  • Giveaway tracker
06 / 06

Your data, your control

Your account keeps everything synced securely across every device you log into — but you're never locked in. Download a complete backup of your inventory, sales, shows, and profile photo as a single file whenever you want, and restore it any time. Prefer your own pricing provider? Drop in your own API keys.

  • Secure account, synced across devices
  • One-file backup & restore
  • Bring-your-own API keys
  • Full control to clear your inventory anytime
